## 🚀 Project Overview

This project explores **Regional Revenue and Margin Analysis** using Microsoft Power BI to transform financial data into meaningful business insights.

Developed as part of **Data Kliq – Cohort 2**, the project demonstrates the application of Business Intelligence techniques, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive data visualization to evaluate business performance across different geographical regions.

The dashboard provides an overview of revenue generation and profit margin performance, helping stakeholders understand regional trends, identify opportunities, and make informed business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue performance across different geographical regions.
- Evaluate profit margins and regional profitability.
- Identify business trends and performance patterns.
- Develop interactive dashboards for financial performance monitoring.
- Apply DAX functions to calculate and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Translate financial data into actionable business insights.

## 📈 Key Areas of Analysis

### 🌍 1. Regional Revenue Analysis
- Examined revenue distribution across geographical regions.
- Compared revenue contributions across different markets.
- Identified high-performing and low-performing regions.

### 💰 2. Profit Margin Analysis
- Evaluated profit margin performance across regions.
- Compared revenue generation against profitability.
- Identified opportunities for improving business margins.

### 📊 3. Business Performance Monitoring
- Developed interactive visualizations for financial reporting.
- Presented key performance indicators to support decision-making.
- Explored regional variations in business performance.
